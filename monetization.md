# DebtShift - SaaS Monetization Strategy

## Pricing Tiers

### FREE (Forever)
**Goal:** Get users hooked, build habit, collect social proof

**Includes:**
- 3 debts max
- Basic dashboard & progress tracking
- Manual payment logging
- Basic calculator
- 5 AI companion messages/month
- 3 lessons in Learn Hub
- Daily motivation quotes
- Basic reminders (3 max)

**Limitations:**
- No charts/visualizations
- No smart allocation
- No negotiation playbooks
- No "Who Owes Me"
- No data export
- Ads shown (non-intrusive)

---

### PRO - $9.99/month ($79.99/year - save 33%)
**Goal:** Core power users, debt-serious individuals

**Everything in Free, plus:**
- **Unlimited debts**
- **Unlimited AI companion** (Shift chat)
- **Full Learn Hub** (all lessons + certificates)
- **Smart Allocation** with AI suggestions
- **All charts & visualizations**
- **Negotiation playbooks** (all creditors)
- **Call scripts generator**
- **Unlimited reminders**
- **Who Owes Me** tracking
- **Budget analysis**
- **Payment planner**
- **Journal & notes**
- **Data export (CSV, PDF)**
- **No ads**
- **Email support**

---

### PRO+ - $19.99/month ($149.99/year - save 37%)
**Goal:** Premium users wanting maximum support

**Everything in Pro, plus:**
- **Priority AI companion** (faster, smarter responses)
- **Custom AI coaching sessions** (weekly personalized advice)
- **Bank sync via Plaid** (auto-import transactions)
- **Advanced insights & predictions**
- **Credit score impact simulator**
- **Settlement calculator & negotiation coach**
- **Balance transfer finder** (best offers curated)
- **Accountability partner matching**
- **Priority support** (chat + email)
- **Early access** to new features

---

### FAMILY - $29.99/month ($239.99/year)
**Goal:** Households tackling debt together

**Everything in Pro+, plus:**
- **Up to 5 family members**
- **Shared household dashboard**
- **Combined debt tracking**
- **Family goals & milestones**
- **Privacy controls** (hide individual debts)
- **Family challenges**

## Paywall Triggers (When to Show Upgrade)

1. **Soft paywall** - User tries to add 4th debt → "Upgrade to Pro for unlimited debts"
2. **AI limit** - 5th message in month → "You've used all free AI chats. Upgrade for unlimited"
3. **Feature discovery** - Taps on Charts/Smart Allocate → Preview with blur + upgrade CTA
4. **Value moment** - After logging 3rd payment → "You're making progress! Unlock insights with Pro"
5. **Milestone** - Hits 10% paid → "Celebrate with Pro features!"

---

## Free Trial Strategy

- **7-day Pro trial** for new users (no credit card required)
- **14-day Pro+ trial** with credit card
- Trial ends → graceful downgrade (keep data, lose features)
- "Miss Pro?" reminder after 3 days of downgrade

## Additional Revenue Streams

### 1. Affiliate Partnerships (Ethical Only)
- Balance transfer card referrals (0% APR cards)
- Debt consolidation loans
- Refinancing partners
- **Commission: $50-150 per qualified lead**
- **Rules:** Only show relevant offers, user must opt-in, never predatory products

### 2. Financial Coaching Marketplace (V2+)
- Connect users with certified debt coaches
- DebtShift takes 15-20% platform fee
- Coaches set own rates ($50-150/session)

### 3. Enterprise/B2B (V3+)
- **Employer Wellness Programs** - Companies offer DebtShift Pro to employees
- Pricing: $3-5/employee/month (bulk discount)
- White-label option for credit unions, banks

### 4. Premium Content
- Expert masterclasses ($19.99 each)
- Downloadable templates & worksheets
- Certification program for debt-free graduates

## Retention Strategies

1. **Streaks** - Don't break your payment logging streak
2. **Progress loss aversion** - "You'll lose access to your charts"
3. **Annual discount** - Save 33% with yearly billing
4. **Win-back** - 50% off for 3 months after churn
5. **Referral program** - Give 1 month Pro, get 1 month Pro

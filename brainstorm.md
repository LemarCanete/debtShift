# DebtShift - Product Roadmap

## Vision
Debt escape system that adapts to real life, provides negotiation tactics, and maintains motivation.

---

## MVP (6-8 weeks)
**Goal:** Validate core value prop with 500 users

### Features
- **Onboarding:** Income type (steady/variable/mixed), income range, essential expenses, goal selection
- **Debt Tracking:** Add/edit debts (name, balance, APR, minimum, creditor)
- **Budgeting:** 
  - Monthly income logging (supports multiple sources)
  - Essential expenses tracking (rent, utilities, food, transport)
  - "Safe to pay extra" calculator (income - essentials - minimums = extra)
  - Variable income mode: adjust recommendations based on actual monthly income
- **Planner:**
  - Monthly payment calendar view
  - Schedule extra payments in advance
  - Set payoff target dates per debt
  - Visual timeline of debt-free journey
- **Reminders:**
  - Payment due alerts (customizable: 1, 3, 7 days before)
  - Weekly check-in prompts
  - Custom reminders (negotiation callbacks, etc.)
  - Push notifications
- **Notes & Journal:**
  - Debt journey journal (wins, setbacks, reflections)
  - Call logs with creditors (date, rep name, outcome)
  - Notes attached to specific debts
  - "Why I started" saved & shown during hard times
- **AI Companion ("Shift"):**
  - Chat interface for questions and guidance
  - Personalized tips based on user's debt situation
  - Motivation boosts and check-ins
  - Explains concepts in plain language
  - Suggests next actions
- **Learn Center:**
  - Bite-sized lessons (2-3 min each)
  - Topics: Debt basics, Budgeting 101, Negotiation tactics, Credit scores, Avoiding relapse
  - Progress tracking with streaks
  - Unlockable advanced content
- **Dashboard:** Total debt, progress %, debt-free date, this month's budget snapshot, upcoming payments
- **Charts & Visualizations:**
  - Debt payoff progress over time (line chart)
  - Debt breakdown by type (pie/donut chart)
  - Monthly payment history (bar chart)
  - Interest saved visualization
  - Projected vs actual progress
- **Daily Motivation:**
  - Daily quote/verse on dashboard
  - Mix of financial wisdom, motivational quotes, and verses
  - Refresh daily, option to save favorites
  - Share to social media
- **Payoff Calculator:** Snowball vs avalanche comparison, extra payment simulator
- **Debt Calculator:**
  - "How much to pay monthly?" - enter debt amount, get payment for 6mo/1yr/2yr/5yr
  - "How long to pay off?" - enter debt + monthly payment, see timeline
  - "How much interest?" - see total interest for different scenarios
  - Compare scenarios side-by-side
  - Save calculations for reference
- **Negotiation Basics:** Top 5 creditor scripts, hardship program info
- **Motivation:** Milestones (10/25/50/75/100%), payment streaks
- **Settings:**
  - Profile management (name, email, photo)
  - Notification preferences
  - Payoff strategy (snowball/avalanche)
  - Currency & date format
  - Theme (dark/light)
  - Data export/import
  - Privacy controls
  - Help & support
- **Contacts (Creditors):**
  - Add/edit creditor profiles
  - Contact info (phone, email, address)
  - Account numbers
  - Notes per creditor
  - Call history log
  - Best times to call
- **History:**
  - Full payment history timeline
  - Balance changes over time
  - Negotiation attempts log
  - Milestone achievements
  - Filter by date/debt/type
- **Money Saving Offers:**
  - Balance transfer card suggestions
  - Refinancing opportunities
  - Debt consolidation options
  - Lower rate alerts
  - Partner discounts
- **Accounts:**
  - All linked accounts overview
  - Bank accounts (future Plaid)
  - Credit cards
  - Loans
  - Account health status
- **Analysis:**
  - Spending patterns
  - Debt-to-income ratio
  - Payment consistency score
  - Interest rate comparison
  - "What if" simulations
- **Impact Score:**
  - Overall financial health score
  - Credit utilization impact
  - Payment history impact
  - Progress trajectory
  - Comparison to goals
- **Who Owes Me:**
  - Track money lent to others
  - Add borrower, amount, date, reason
  - Set reminders to follow up
  - Mark as paid/partial/written off
  - Total owed to you
- **Insights:**
  - AI-generated weekly insights
  - Spending anomalies
  - Optimization suggestions
  - Celebration of wins
  - Risk alerts
- **Countdown:**
  - Debt-free date countdown (days/months/years)
  - Per-debt countdown timers
  - Milestone countdowns
  - Visual progress timeline
  - Share countdown to social
- **Smart Money Allocation:**
  - Auto-divide income across debts (configurable in settings)
  - AI-suggested allocation based on strategy (snowball/avalanche/hybrid)
  - "I have $X extra" - shows recommended split
  - Drag-to-adjust sliders for manual tweaking
  - Save custom allocation templates
  - One-tap apply suggested allocation
  - Preview impact on payoff dates before confirming

### Success Criteria
- 200 users complete onboarding
- 50 users log a payment
- 20 users view negotiation scripts
- NPS > 40

---

## V1 (4-6 weeks post-MVP)
**Goal:** Launch paid tier at $9.99/mo

### Additions
- **Enhanced Budgeting:**
  - Expense categories with targets
  - "Flush month" detection - auto-suggest extra payments
  - "Lean month" mode - safety recommendations
  - Emergency fund tracking
  - Side hustle income tagging
- Variable income mode with smart payment suggestions
- 15+ creditor playbooks
- Settlement calculator
- Balance transfer optimizer
- Weekly check-ins with mood tracking
- Achievement badges
- Push notifications

---

## V2 (3-4 months post-V1)
**Goal:** AI personalization + community

### Additions
- Bank sync via Plaid
- AI-powered payment recommendations
- Custom script generator
- Anonymous community progress sharing
- Accountability partners

---

## V3+ (6-12 months)
- Web app companion
- B2B: Employer wellness programs, credit union white-label
- Credit score integration

---

## Anti-Features (Won't Build)
- Credit card marketplace
- Predatory loan ads
- Public comparison/shaming
- Complex features requiring tutorials

---

## North Star Metric
**Total debt paid off by DebtShift users**
